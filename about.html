Our Story

Since 2002

“Tohfet Al Benaa was registered as a trade license in December 2002. Since then, general contracting has stayed the core of the business — one site at a time.”

Today the company holds a Fourth-Category engineering classification from the Department of Municipalities and Transport, is registered for VAT with the Federal Tax Authority, and is a member of the Abu Dhabi Chamber of Commerce and Industry. Behind those credentials sits a straightforward track record: over 100 completed projects across the company's history, most of them private villas built from an empty plot through to a full municipal completion certificate.

How We Work

Our Process

Five stages, applied consistently from a villa extension to a full ground-up build.

01

Consultation

We meet on site or remotely to understand scope, budget and timeline.

02

Licensing & Design

Drawings are coordinated with our consultants and submitted for municipal approval.

03

Site Mobilisation

Our crews, materials and equipment mobilise under a fixed contract and schedule.

04

Construction

Structural, façade and finishing works proceed under continuous site supervision.

05

Handover

Municipal completion certification is secured and the finished project is handed over.
